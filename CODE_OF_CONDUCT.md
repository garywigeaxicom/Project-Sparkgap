# Code of Conduct

Project Sparkgap is a public learning and design project. Participants are expected to contribute with respect, curiosity, and care for the people affected by AI systems.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome different levels of experience and perspectives.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Make room for accessibility, cultural, linguistic, and disciplinary differences.
- Take safety, privacy, and potential downstream harm seriously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and sharing private or sensitive information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's designated contact channel. Include enough context to investigate, but do not include secrets or unnecessary personal information. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove content, pause participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior conflicts with this code.
